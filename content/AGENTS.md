# Content integrity

`uses.ts` is the authoritative gear history, combining original records with Josh’s explicit updates. UI labels may be derived, but never silently alter item names, specs, acquire dates, or retirement dates. Dates may have month or day precision; preserve the precision supplied by Josh. Test historical snapshots and same-month replacements when changing derivation logic.

Personal biography and résumé facts come from the supplied handoff, explicit user corrections, or verified sources recorded with the content. Handoff `JOBS` dates, titles, and blurbs are placeholders and must not be copied as facts. Never revive facts from the old Gatsby pages.

`artist_genres.json` is the primary artist genre catalog; Spotify supplies genres only when this file has no usable labels for that artist. Track only artists with more than 25 cumulative recorded listens, pruning lower-count entries during local discovery. Omit Christmas genres, separate `christian` from compound styles, and replace `worship` and `ccm` with `christian`, preserving the remaining style and deduplicating labels. It contains artist metadata only, with ordered genres and Spotify, Codex, or manual provenance. Blank entries with `source: null` await classification; blank Codex entries record an attempted but uncertain identification. Keep listening counts, source events, example tracks, and credentials out of this file. Spotify syncs and scheduled jobs must never overwrite it.

For artists with one saved genre, preserve that genre first and ask Codex for 1-2 additions. Apply the same rules before appending at most two distinct tags. Preserve the original source/date and record additive Codex provenance in `genreExpansion`, including unsuccessful attempts. Subsequent local discovery must preserve these additions.
