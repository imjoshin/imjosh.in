# Content integrity

`uses.ts` is preserved from the original repository and is the authoritative gear history. UI labels may be derived, but never silently alter item names, specs, acquire dates, or retirement dates. Test historical snapshots and same-month replacements when changing derivation logic.

Personal biography and résumé facts come from the supplied handoff, explicit user corrections, or verified sources recorded with the content. Handoff `JOBS` dates, titles, and blurbs are placeholders and must not be copied as facts. Never revive facts from the old Gatsby pages.
